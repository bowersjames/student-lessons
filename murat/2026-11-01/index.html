First the topic, then the delivery

  1. Silence for three seconds and the question ends. In practice, never stop more than two.
  2. Pause between groups, not inside them. Stopping mid-group loses the words that come after.
  3. Steady, not fast. Rushed and unclear still loses pronunciation marks.
  4. Never start again. Restarting costs more marks than the mistake did.
  5. Stress the key words, link inside the group. That smooth sound is what fluent means to the marker.

Hold it in groups

  1. Most test sentences have three beats. Who, did what, when, where or why. Say each beat as one group.
WhoDid whatWhen, where or why
The universityopened a new librarylast September
Most studentspassed the exambecause the course was well taught
  1. A new group often starts at before, because, in, at or which.
  2. Lost a word? Keep going. Say a close word or drop it. Never start again.

The image template

  1. This [chart / map / image] shows [the title, word for word].
  1. The highest value is [name], at about [number].
  2. The lowest is [name], at about [number].
  3. Overall, it shows [rising / falling / uneven].

Part 4

Retell Lecture

Catch five key words, then retell with a frame.

22

The Retell Lecture frame

  1. The lecture was about [topic word].
  2. In the beginning the speaker explained [fact 1].
  3. And then [fact 2]. Later [fact 3].
  4. In conclusion, [the main idea].

Part 5

Respond to a Situation

Echo the situation back, then ask politely.

26

The Respond to a Situation frame

  1. Say the situation back, but you becomes I. "I am [their description of you]."
  2. "I fully understand how important this is."
  3. "I would really appreciate it if you could [what you want]."
27

Covering a shift

Listen to and read a description of a situation. Then answer a question about it with an extended response.

Your colleague Sam asks you to cover their shift on Saturday. You have already planned to visit your family that day. What do you say to Sam?

28

Group presentation

Listen to and read a description of a situation. Then answer a question about it with an extended response.

You are working on a group project at university. You have not finished your part, and the presentation is next week. What do you say to the group?

29

Also on your page

  1. Summarise Group Discussion. While you listen, catch one claim per speaker. Then, "The speakers discussed [topic]. The first said [claim]. The second [agreed / disagreed], adding [claim]. The third said [claim]. Overall, the main point was [shared idea]."
  2. Write from Dictation. No frame. Write the meaning chunks first, fix the spelling last. Every correct word is a point.
  3. Answer Short Question. The cheapest marks on the page. One word or a few words, never a sentence.
30

What you take away today

Repeat Sentence, hold the groups, not the words. Lost a word? Keep going.

Describe Image, the title word for word, then highest and lowest or biggest and smallest. Arrows, starts as, then, finally.

Read Aloud, use the 35 seconds to find the words to stress and the hard words.
