Describe Image

Task rules

  • Study the image for 25 seconds.
  • Wait for the tone.
  • Speak for up to 40 seconds in your own words.
  • Choose what matters most and finish cleanly.

What matters

  • Content
  • Pronunciation
  • Oral Fluency
  • Relevant information, relationships, logical detail, intelligible natural speech and a clean finish.

Choose, connect, finish

Best practices

  • Choose one overall message.
  • Select 2–3 useful details.
  • Show one relationship or comparison.
  • Do not list everything.

Planning

  • For a simple image, plan mentally.
  • For a complex image, use 3–5 handwritten keywords.
  • Use a main message, two details and one relationship.

Turn analysis into a clear response

Overall, both revenue and costs increased across the year, while revenue remained higher. The gap widened in Q3 before narrowing in Q4.

  • Overall, the image shows…
  • One key feature is…
  • Compared with…, …
  • Overall, …
  • A flexible guide, not a script.
