Fix the End

A great End answers 3 questions.

  • Is the problem finished?
  • How did I feel, and why?
  • What changed?

Each of the next 3 stories has a weak End. Find what is missing. Then write a better End on paper.

Story 1

On Saturday, I lost my phone at South Bank. I looked everywhere, and I started to panic. Then a woman tapped me on the shoulder.

End

I felt relieved because she was so kind, and I smiled all the way home. Now, I always check my bag twice.

Which one is missing? Tap it.

Now write a better End

On paper, 3 or 4 sentences. Draw the 3 symbols next to the sentences that answer each question.

Stuck?

The woman ______.
I felt ______ because ______.
Now, when I ______, I ______.

The woman held up my phone. She found it on a bench near the river. I felt so relieved because all my photos were on it. Now, I always check my bag twice before I leave.

Story 2

One year after the little prince left, I flew back to the desert. I sat under his star and waited all night. Just before morning, I heard a small laugh behind me.

End

When I turned around, the little prince was there. He told me his rose was safe. Now, I look at the stars every night.

Which one is missing? Tap it.

Now write a better End

On paper, 3 or 4 sentences. You are the pilot, so write "I". Draw the 3 symbols next to the sentences that answer each question.

Stuck?

When I turned around, ______.
I felt ______ because ______.
Now, when I ______, I ______.

When I turned around, the little prince was there, and he was laughing. He told me his rose was safe. I felt so happy that I cried, because I was not alone any more. Now, when I look at the stars, I hear him laugh.

Story 3

Last term, I had to give a speech to my whole class. My hands were shaking, and I forgot my first line. Then my friend smiled at me from the front row.

End

I remembered the words and finished my speech. Everyone clapped. I felt proud because I didn't give up.

Which one is missing? Tap it.

Now write a better End

On paper, 3 or 4 sentences. Draw the 3 symbols next to the sentences that answer each question.

Stuck?

When I saw my friend, ______.
I felt ______ because ______.
Now, when I ______, I ______.

When I saw my friend smile, I remembered my words and finished the speech. Everyone clapped. I felt proud because I didn't give up. Now, I am not so scared when I speak in class.
